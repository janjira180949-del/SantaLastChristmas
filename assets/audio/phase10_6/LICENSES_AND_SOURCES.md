The official [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) permits copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use. Every manifest row records these permissions, based on its source dedication. This is source-license verification, not an independent guarantee that a publisher owns every possible underlying right. No conflicting terms were found for selected attachments. Unknown/conflicting non-CC0 search results were not selected.

`LICENSE_EVIDENCE.json` preserves creator names, verified page URLs, license fields, download links, date, page SHA-256 and scope. Original page snapshots remain under project-local build evidence; only license metadata is reproduced here. Included archive INFO/readme files corroborate Juhani Junkala's CC0 dedication and Corsica_S's explicit permission for Iwan Gabovitch's footstep extraction.

LEGIT Audio's CC0 grant applies only to its free OpenGameArt samples. No paid-pack audio was obtained. Those continuous room-tone recordings are spectrally shaped into environmental textures; this is disclosed, not portrayed as a recording of a real magical crystal. Fantozzi's established OGA curator explicitly identifies the original Freesound pack and CC0 origin; the Freesound page returned403 during verification. The evidence records that access limitation instead of claiming independent Freesound verification.

New ambience arrangement, loop editing and contact extraction are technical/sound-design adaptations of the credited recordings, released under CC0 to the extent any new copyright arises. They are not newly recorded field audio or a newly composed score. All original creators retain credit. `CREDITS.txt` is ready for voluntary acknowledgment in a future authorized credits update; the game credits have not been edited.
